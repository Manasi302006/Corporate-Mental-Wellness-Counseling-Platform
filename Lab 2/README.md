## 1. Project Overview

The Corporate Mental Wellness & Counseling Platform is intended to provide employees with a confidential and accessible way to assess their stress levels, explore counseling services, schedule sessions with licensed therapists, and track personal wellness indicators.

The system emphasizes employee privacy, secure handling of sensitive information, anonymized identity management, and confidential communication between employees and therapists.

## 2. Project Objectives

* Support confidential stress assessments.
* Protect employee identity through anonymized identifiers.
* Allow employees to browse therapists and schedule counseling sessions.
* Support secure clinical progress notes.
* Enable optional personal wellness tracking.
* Address data encryption, privacy, and scheduling reliability.

## 4. Functional Scope

1. Generate an anonymized employee pseudo-ID.
2. Complete a confidential stress assessment.
3. Browse therapists and schedule confidential counseling sessions.
4. Record anonymized clinical progress notes.
5. Track personal wellness indicators.

## 5. Non-Functional Scope

* Accurate timezone conversion for session scheduling.
* Secure data storage and transmission.
* Privacy and confidentiality of employee and wellness information.
* Reliability and appropriate access control.
